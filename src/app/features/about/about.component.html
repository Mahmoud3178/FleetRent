Our story

From one branch to a nationwide fleet

FleetRent began as a small operation trying to solve a simple problem: renting a car shouldn't feel like a negotiation. Every price is calculated up front, every car is inspected before handover, and every booking is confirmed instantly — no phone calls, no waiting rooms.

Today we manage a growing fleet across multiple branches, with a platform built to keep the whole cycle — booking, pickup, and return — fast and transparent for both renters and our operations team.
